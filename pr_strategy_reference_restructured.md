### PR Strategy — Two Paths (the Path A / Path B framework)

**The Problem:**
- Phase 3 (gradual normalisation) has a HARD GATE: finalisation. Divorce isn't done.
- Phase 2 (same-event appearances) doesn't work because they have no shared events, no shared work, no common social circles outside awards/festival season.
- If there is no natural overlap, you cannot "normalise through proximity."

**Path A — Stay Completely Separate (Low Risk / Slow):**
- No shared visibility at all. Independent normality on both sides.
- Safe short-term. But when they eventually appear together = revelation, not continuation. Explosive spike because no bridge built.

**Path B — Create Controlled Overlap (Higher Skill / Better Long-Term):**
- Manufacture believable shared spaces slowly. Not fake — plausible.
- Shared connector (someone like Chace who sits in both worlds).
- Private mixed-group dinners first → small visible overlaps → occasional public proximity.
- Once every few weeks maximum. Slow enough to look natural.
- Riskier short-term but much safer long-term — creates continuity.

**Her Team vs His Team:**
- Her team: keep hiding for two more years. The approach protects her reputation. One lost campaign better than confirmed relationship while divorce unfinished.
- His team: recommends Path B. Hiding creates worse explosion later.

**Key Line:**
- "If the world can't see how they'd meet — then the moment they're seen together, it becomes a revelation instead of a continuation."
