## [BEAUTY LINE]: 18-Month Master Blueprint

**Concept:** Mass prestige hybrid skincare-meets-makeup. Anonymous LLC, trusted proxy as public B2B face. Stealth operation — no public connection to Aoibhéann until post-exclusivity.

**Idea surfaced:** October 2026 (Part 3 Ch1)
**Oct 2026 – May 2027:** Exploring, deciding path. Wanted luxury — consultant said impossible (reputation risk with older women, public profile damage). Mass prestige also challenged (LA social butterfly image contracted, dating man in late 40s with kids not aspirational). Both paths damaged by public profile.
**Mass prestige decision locked:** Late May / June 2027. Only path left.
**Phase 1 starts:** ~June 2027
**Armani Beauty exclusivity expires:** End of 2027. She is NOT renewing. Launch cannot happen until exclusivity ends.

### Phase 1: Foundation & The "Data Room" (~Jun – Aug 2027)
- Attorney sets up anonymous LLC, drafts NDAs
- Trusted proxy appointed as public face for B2B operations
- Initial capital from her own money — legal fees, lab retainers
- Pitch deck and secure virtual Data Room built
- Meets privately with cosmetic chemist/beauty consultant to define the "Hero" product
- Finalises skincare actives (e.g., niacinamide, hyaluronic acid) and initial shade range for skin tint or hybrid blush

### Phase 2: Stealth R&D & VC Pitching (~Sep – Nov 2027)
- Closed-door meetings with beauty-focused VC firms under strict NDAs
- Pitches hybrid concept and bicoastal market advantage for Seed Round
- Proxy engages labs: New Jersey (pigment experts) and California (clean skincare experts)
- Labs produce bench samples — she tests privately
- Packaging sourcing begins: airless pumps, UV-coated glass. Custom tooling takes up to 16 weeks

### Phase 3: The Green Light & Mass Production (~Dec 2027 – Feb 2028)
- Armani exclusivity expires end of 2027 — legally clear
- VC term sheets signed, Seed Round capital lands
- Bench samples approved → mandatory stability and challenge testing begins (12-week process)
- Custom packaging deposits paid with VC money
- Creative agency hired for brand name, typography, packaging design

### Phase 4: Brand Building & Operations (~Mar – Jun 2028)
- Campaign shoot: she books photographers, shoots editorial campaign featuring herself
- Executive team hired (VP of Product, Head of Marketing)
- 3PL partner secured — fulfillment warehouses on both coasts
- Filling and assembly begins once stability testing clears

### Phase 5: Pre-Launch & Retail Pitching (~Jul – Sep 2028)
- Pitches to Sephora ("Scouted") or Ulta ("Sparked") for exclusive retail rollout
- D2C Shopify site built
- Product seeded to celebrity makeup artists, estheticians, beauty editors

### Phase 6: Go-To-Market Launch (~Oct – Dec 2028)
- Finished products shipped to 3PL warehouses in NY and CA
- Press embargo lifts — WWD, Vogue, Business of Fashion
- Launch event: experiential masterclass in LA or NY
- D2C website goes live

### Where She Is in June 2027
**End of Phase 1, entering Phase 2.** The foundational work ran parallel to the path decision: Armani contract reviewed with lawyers, consultation meetings on strategy/formulation/lab locations, LLC set up, financial consultants on business and investment strategy. Mass prestige now locked as only viable path. She is now looking for investors and locking down labs and formulas. Lawyers and consultants NYC-based. Labs she needs to engage — particularly clean skincare experts — are in California. VC pitching about to begin.
