## Chapter 1 — The Professional Distance
*June/July 2025 — Late October 2025*
*POV: Primarily Aoibhéann, one brief Jensen beat before the first meeting*

---

### Beat 1 — The Modelling Call
*Santa Monica Canyon house, morning*

Open on Aoibhéann in her daily world. A call about a campaign shoot — Cassidy relaying a brief she has notes on. The reader enters through the public version of her — the model, the platform, the selective work at 35. Small details establish the life she's built: the house, the necklace, the ease of someone who has been doing this long enough that nothing requires performance.

The dry humour surfaces in how she handles something that needs managing. The accent present because she's comfortable. This is the version of her the headlines know — and already the reader can see it's not the whole picture.

The call ends. A message from Cassidy or Richard Weiss pulls her attention toward the charity — specifically the ambassador decision she's been circling.

---

### Beat 2 — The Family Dinner
*Jack's LA house, evening*

Jack, Stephanie, John, Declan, Shannon. An ordinary family dinner — not a special occasion, just the specific texture of this family around a table.

The dynamics establish themselves without explanation. John deferring to Jack. Shannon performing for Stephanie. Declan warm toward Aoibhéann in his specific way — present, deflecting things that get pointed before they land.

The charity comes up. Jack asks in the way he asks — interested enough to engage, containing a quiet reservation. Then — she mentions the Dodger Stadium. Already secured. Through the charity's connections and her own. The reader understands what this means even if the family's reaction is mixed. Jack's response is telling — either genuine acknowledgment that she pulled something significant off, or a qualified approval that still contains a reservation. Stephanie's response is perfectly managed. Declan is genuinely pleased.

The public image comment arrives — Shannon or something Jack saw online, delivered without drama, just present. Aoibhéann's response: she doesn't shrink, doesn't rise to it. Handles it with the dry economy of someone who has been navigating this table her whole life. Declan redirects. The dinner continues.

The reader understands her position in this family before anything has been explained.

---

### Beat 3 — The Charity Work and Her Perception of Jensen
*Charity office / Santa Monica Canyon house, next day*

The work beat. Richard Weiss, Cassidy, the event in its current planning state. The existing concept: a Legends vs Celebrity baseball game at Dodger Stadium — Richard's contribution, shaped by the team's sports world connections through Tom. A strong concept. A legitimate event. Missing something.

The ambassador decision has been circling. She's landed on Jensen Ackles. The specific reasoning: his fanbase's documented relationship with mental health conversations, his genuine involvement through Jared Padalecki, his mainstream American profile, Chaos Machine's Amazon relationship.

Her team pulls the research. She reviews it. Supernatural, Dean Winchester, The Boys/Soldier Boy, Countdown upcoming and looking promising — his profile on an upward trajectory. The production company. The universal professional reputation she almost distrusts because it's too consistent. Married, three kids, Texas. The gossip blog narrative from her twenties flagged as potential press consideration.

Her conclusion: useful ambassador, probably straightforward to manage. She's worked with talent before. She knows how this goes.

Decision made. Her team approaches his team.

Brief montage of what follows — phone conferences, emails, the professional back and forth across several weeks. The charity vision presented. His team engaged. A first meeting arranged for when he's in LA for the Countdown premiere press.

---

### Beat 4 — His Perception
*Brief Jensen POV — day of the meeting*

His team has briefed him. The Connolly name noted. The modelling career — high fashion, Victoria's Secret, selective campaigns now. The charity work — credible but still building. The gossip blog narrative flagged. Platform and following assessed as genuinely useful.

His conclusion going in: well connected model from a prominent family moving into philanthropy. Knows how the industry works. He's met versions of this profile before.

The Countdown premiere press has been a good week. The show feels like it's in a strong position. He's in an optimistic professional headspace walking into this meeting.

He arrives at Jack Connolly's office building.

---

### Beat 5 — The First Meeting
*Jack's offices, LA — July 2025*

Richard Weiss and Aoibhéann are already there when Jensen arrives. Richard at the table, notes in front of him, the institutional weight of someone who has been doing this a long time. Aoibhéann slightly apart — standing, reviewing something, completely focused. Not performing the wait.

A brief internal beat from her as Jensen walks in — the gap between the research version and the in person version registering before she can examine it. Filed immediately. She has a meeting to run.

It starts as Richard's meeting in format — he makes the introductions, establishes the charity's history and credibility, the foundation that was there before she arrived. Jensen is engaged, respectful, asking the right questions.

And then Aoibhéann starts talking about the event.

The transition happens without drama. Richard doesn't cede the floor — she simply fills it. Her passion for the cause, the specific vision for what the event can be, the logic of why it matters and why this venue and this moment and this ambassador are the right combination. She's not performing conviction. She has it. The room reorganises itself around her without anyone deciding that should happen.

Richard lets it happen. That's its own telling detail — he's not threatened, he facilitates. This is how they always work together. Jensen watches the transition and understands something about both of them before anyone explains it.

She's sold him within minutes. The pitch shifts into brainstorm.

He engages genuinely, asks specific questions. Then — he looks at the existing structure and names what's missing. The event has the sporting element, the venue, the cause. But what sets it apart from every other celebrity charity sports game? What gives it mainstream appeal that reaches beyond the usual audience?

He suggests a band at the end of the day. Practical, direct.

She takes it and immediately goes further. Why just one band. Why just the end of the day. The music element is big enough to be its own thing — its own evening, its own audience, its own artists who speak specifically to the mental health conversation.

From there it builds between them — the second day emerging naturally. The evening concert becoming the anchor of Day Two. Richard raises his reservations in real time — too ambitious, logistics double, budget implications, they're still building. She hears him out across the table and overrides it without hesitation. The venue is there, the ambassador is on board, she knows this world. Richard notes it and moves on. Jensen watches that exchange too.

Neither he nor Aoibhéann said let's make this a two day event. They just built it together until it was.

*His read of her:* Not what the research suggested. The specific intelligence. The authority over something that is clearly hers — not given to her, not her father's connections made visible, actually hers. She took his spark and built something he hadn't seen yet. The way she overrode Richard without making it a confrontation. The passion that filled the room without performing. That's the first thing that files somewhere specific without his permission.

*Her read of him:* Easier to work with than expected. The music suggestion landed exactly where it needed to. He's not going to be passive — he engaged, he contributed something real. She notes Richard watching Jensen with the specific satisfaction of someone who made the right call. Files Jensen appropriately and moves on. Useful. Probably straightforward.

The meeting ends. Both leave with their professional assessments intact. Neither is aware that anything has shifted.

---

### Beat 6 — The First Direct Contact
*Late summer/early autumn 2025*

She secures the second day at Dodger Stadium. A significant logistical win — weeks of negotiation, the right connections, the specific ask that required her and the team's combined relationships.

When it comes through she fires off a direct email to Jensen before she's thought about whether to go through teams. The impulse of someone who just landed something and needs to tell the person it affects most.

He responds directly. Quickly. More quickly than she expected.

The buffer drops. Not deliberately. The news was too good to wait for the proper channel.

A second direct contact follows when one of them secures a significant artist for the evening concert. Good news travels between them before it travels through teams. The pattern established.

---

### Beat 7 — The Announcement and the Countdown Cancellation
*Sept/Oct 2025 — Late October 2025*

**The announcement — Sept/Oct:**
Written press only. A statement, quotes from both, one photograph. Their names connected publicly for the first time. The event real to the world now.

Brief beat of her watching the coverage — the press writing about her in the way the press always writes about her even in a professional context. The gossip blog version reaching for the familiar narrative alongside the legitimate coverage. She notes it and moves on. The coverage is positive where it matters. His fanbase engages with the mental health angle exactly as she predicted.

**The Countdown cancellation — late October:**
She sees it through the news or Cassidy flags it. A moment of assessment: does this change anything for the event? She goes through it practically — nine months away, his profile strong enough to absorb it, the Amazon relationship through Vought Rising actually stronger. Her conclusion: it changes nothing.

A brief note to Cassidy. They continue.

Then she finds the Instagram video.

She watches him talk about the cancellation to his audience. Not Jensen Ackles the ambassador, not the research version, not the man she pitched to in July. Just him — handling something that clearly cost him something with the specific grace of someone who is genuinely disappointed and genuinely grateful simultaneously. The two things coexisting without performance. He talks about the crew and the cast before he mentions the audience. The Texas in his voice when he's not in a press context.

She watches it once. Watches it again.

Files it under professional due diligence — she should know how he handles adversity publicly, it's relevant to the event. That's what she tells herself.

What she doesn't know: what the cancellation actually cost him. The private landscape shifting underneath the professional surface she can see clearly.

The chapter ends with both of them moving forward — her building something, him managing something — their names publicly linked and their private realities entirely separate.

## Chapter 2 — The Shift
*Late October 2025 — 3rd Week January 2026*
*POV: Dual — weighted toward her for the contact and recruitment, his POV for the private world. Conversations shown rather than internal monologue.*

---

### Beat 1 — Two Worlds, One Timeline
*Late October — November 2025*

*His world:*
The marriage ending over phone calls. Not one conversation — several across October and November, each one arriving a little closer to the conclusion without either of them naming it directly. He's in Toronto filming. She's in Connecticut with the kids. The distance that was supposed to be the rethink time has done its work — neither of them missed the other as a spouse. It arrives gradually. No single call where they say it's over. Just the accumulation of calls where neither of them is pretending anymore.

He tells his family — Donna first. From Toronto, on the phone, because that's how it works when your parents are in Texas and you're in Canada. The conversation happens the way his important conversations always happen — quietly, privately, completely. Josh and Mackenzie told. No public presence, no leaks. Just family knowing.

JDM finds out through Hilarie almost immediately. Danneel has been talking to Hilarie throughout — not a single revelation but the same gradual arrival, shared in real time with her closest friend. JDM reaches out to Jensen. The conversation that follows is the one that matters. JDM has been through something closer to this than most people Jensen knows. He doesn't offer advice unless asked. He asks the right questions. Jensen answers them honestly enough.

Jared doesn't know yet. Misha doesn't know yet. That comes later, in its own time.

*Her world:*
Direct contact is now established. Good news goes between them without teams.

The second day at Dodger Stadium confirmed — she calls him directly. He responds quickly.

OneRepublic confirmed — her call, her music world connection. She tells him.
Keith Urban confirmed — his call, his Nashville connection. He tells her.

The panel and VIP concept emerges from a November call — she's thinking about what Day One looks like beyond the baseball game. How to keep the mental health cause central. The structured experiences, the panels, the behind the scenes access. He engages genuinely. The call runs the length it needs to and no longer. Professional, purposeful. No lingering.

Updates in passing across November and December — Jared confirmed, Shaun confirmed, Miles confirmed, Lily confirmed. The roster taking shape in the background of everything else.

---

### Beat 2 — The Baseball Problem
*November — December 2025*

She makes a call to one of the Legends. A personal pitch — the kind of direct conversation that closes things that emails don't.

It becomes very quickly apparent that she has no idea who he is. Or what position he played. Or what his career actually meant. She tries to cover it. Makes it worse. The Legend is gracious. She gets off the call and tells Cassidy what happened with the specific self-awareness of someone who finds herself genuinely funny.

This is the first visible consequence of building a charity event around a sport she has never watched. It won't be the last.

Her sports are rugby, soccer and hurling. Her brothers played NFL — she knows that world, she knows those players. She chose baseball because Dodger Stadium was right and the cause mattered and she figured the rest out as she went. Cassidy is dispatched to brief her before every subsequent baseball related call.

This becomes a recurring thread — her tenacity in building something around a sport she knows nothing about, surfacing in small specific moments across the timeline. Jensen finds it specifically funny when he finds out. A woman who runs meetings with complete authority and built a two day event at Dodger Stadium and genuinely doesn't know what an ERA is.

---

### Beat 3 — The NYC Meeting
*Mid to late December 2025 — Jensen lands in NYC before driving to CT*

He flies from Toronto to New York before heading to Connecticut for Christmas. A charity meeting — legitimate, scheduled, something that needs both of them in person before the year ends.

The meeting does what meetings do. Richard or Cassidy present for part of it. The work finishes.

They end up at dinner. Not planned — the meeting ran late, neither of them has eaten, the simplest version of what happens next. Richard or Cassidy already gone. Just the two of them across a table in a restaurant neither of them chose with any particular thought.

The conversation stops being about the event. Not dramatically — the way conversations shift when two people who have been talking on the phone for months are suddenly in person and the professional reason for being there has run its course. Something about his kids surfaces — not the separation, not the weight of it, just something real about his life that isn't the charity. She tells him something about her Christmas plans — the Connolly family, the East Coast, said with the specific dry economy that tells him more about how she feels about it than a longer explanation would.

Nothing happens. They eat, they talk, he drives to Connecticut. She goes back to Jack's apartment or wherever she's staying.

Someone sees them. A server, another diner, someone who recognises one or both of them. Not a photograph — just a memory. A sighting that means nothing in December 2025 and will mean everything when someone goes looking in July 2026.

---

### Beat 4 — Christmas
*December 2025*

*His world — shown through conversation with JDM:*
He's home. The house in Connecticut running exactly as it always has — breakfast, school runs, the kids' routines intact. He and Danneel holding the shape of the family through December because JJ and the twins deserve one more intact Christmas before everything changes.

A conversation with JDM at some point in December. Not long. JDM asks how he's doing. Jensen answers honestly enough. JDM says something useful about the kids — something that comes from having been through his own version of this. Jensen hears it.

*Her world:*
The East Coast. The Connolly Christmas — New York, the family assembled. She navigates it with the specific economy of someone who has been doing it her whole life. Rob finds her at some point in the evening. They end up talking after everyone else has gone to bed.

---

### Beat 5 — January 5th
*Shown through a conversation with Josh*

They tell the kids in the first days of January. Both parents present. JJ needs a real conversation — she's twelve, she understands, she has questions. The twins need presence more than explanation.

He has until January 5th.

He calls Josh before he leaves — or from the airport. His brother who has no public presence, who will keep it completely private, who is simply his brother. The conversation where Jensen can say what he actually feels without managing it for anyone. What it was like to tell the kids. What getting on this plane feels like. Josh listens the way brothers listen — not fixing anything, just there.

The drawings in his pocket. The plane.

A call with Misha possibly — later, maybe, when he's ready to talk about the practical navigation rather than just the weight of it. But not yet.

---

### Beat 6 — The Chace Crawford Call
*Early January 2026 — he's just left*

She's building the celebrity players list. She calls Chace Crawford. Chace tells her Jensen already reached out.

She calls Jensen — or it comes up on their next call.

*Fuck, you're quick. You even got to my friends before I did.*

He laughs. Genuinely. He's been in Toronto for days, carrying January 5th, and something simply feels good for the first time since Christmas. She made him laugh without knowing he needed it.

She moves to the next item. He files it somewhere. The call continues professionally. The first crack in the purely professional register. A small door neither of them has walked through yet.

She sees his airport Instagram post from when he left — something generic that places him there. She doesn't interact with it. Just sees it. Notes it. Knows he's back in Toronto.

---

### Beat 7 — The Hilarie and JDM Call
*Mid to late January 2026*

A routine call about the event. She asks about confirming JDM and Hilarie's commitments.

He hesitates. Says they're thinking about pulling out.

She asks why.

He answers without thinking — because he's just found out and the hurt and frustration come out before he can manage them.

*Because they don't want to take sides.*

She hears everything in that word. Sides. She knows exactly what sides means. She grew up as the child in the middle of two sides. She knows the specific architecture of a separation from the inside.

She doesn't address it directly. Keeps it professional. Gives him the out.

Then asks — simply — if he's okay. Says she hopes things work out for him. Adds warmly that if JDM and Hilarie do pull out it won't be an issue, just to keep her updated. That she'll sort it.

He says thank you. Means it beyond the event.

Then she completely changes the subject.

She tells him about the Legends call. The one where it became very clear she has no idea about baseball. Tells it on herself — completely self-deprecating, genuinely funny. The Legend was gracious. She was not her best self.

He laughs again. Properly.

The call ends differently than it started. She moved them through it. He notices that.

He watches something she posted later — a charity update, something from her daily world. Doesn't interact with it. Just watches. The reader sees it. Neither of them has named what this is yet.

---

### Beat 8 — En Route to New York
*3rd week January 2026*

She knows something now that she didn't know before the last call. She doesn't know exactly how much she knows or what to do with it. She goes to New York.

He's carrying January 5th and the Hilarie and JDM situation and the specific relief of having laughed twice in calls with her when he needed it. He goes to New York.

The chapter ends in transit. Both of them moving toward the same city. Toward the day that will change what this is.

## Chapter 3 — New York
*January 22nd 2026 — one day*
*POV: Primarily Aoibhéann, brief Jensen beats at the shoot and the corridor*

---

### Beat 1 — 6:10 AM — The Car to GMA
*Her POV*

Early. The city still dark. She's in the car alone — Cassidy meeting her there. The day mapped out in her head before it starts. She knows what she knows from the Hilarie and JDM call. She hasn't decided what to do with it. She goes to GMA.

---

### Beat 2 — 6:30 AM — The Damp Hair Moment
*His POV*

She knocks on his dressing room door to say good morning. Damp hair, no makeup, whatever she threw on. Running through the segment in her head. Completely unbothered by being seen like this — not performing nonchalance, genuinely not registering it as a problem.

He answers the door and she's already talking about the segment.

He wasn't prepared for this version of her. Not because she looks better or worse than any other version. Because she is more completely herself than he has seen her and has no idea that's what she's showing him. Every version of her he's catalogued up to this point has been considered in some way. This one just is.

She moves on to the next thing. He stands in the doorway for a moment after she's gone.

---

### Beat 3 — 6:30–7:00 AM — Hair and Makeup
*Her POV*

The GMA glam team. Producer comes in — confirms segment topics, charity details, pronunciation checks, mic placement. The professional machinery of morning television running around both of them.

She watches him in the mirror — how he handles the makeup chair, the producer's questions, the small talk with the glam team. The same with everyone. She's been told this about him and observed it across months of contact. Seeing it in person in a context this ordinary confirms something she didn't need confirming.

---

### Beat 4 — ~7:25 AM — GMA Segment
*Her POV*

Live. Four minutes on air. The charity, the event, the why. He credits her — says it's her idea, her vision, her event. She redirects to the cause, mentions Richard and the team, keeps the charity the subject rather than herself.

Performing as a unit in front of a camera is its own specific intimacy. You have to trust someone to do it well. By the time they're off mic she understands why the day feels different to the meetings and the calls. Proximity and performance together produce something that neither produces alone.

---

### Beat 5 — 8:05 AM — The Car to Kelly and Mark
*Her POV — the Radio Company beat*

They share the car. Ten minutes across the city.

Something comes up in conversation — the concert lineup, the music element, something that leads there naturally. She mentions Radio Company. Not as a pitch. Just — it came up.

He goes quiet for a beat. Then asks how she knows about it.

She says something flat — she looked. Did her research.

He doesn't say anything else about it immediately. But something shifted. She went past the obvious version of him and he felt it.

The car pulls up. They get out. The moment doesn't get examined.

---
