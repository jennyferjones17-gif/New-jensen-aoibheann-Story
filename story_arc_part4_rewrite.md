## Part Four Operating Principle

Real worlds collide. The private rebuild meets the mess of actual life — relationship histories, families, friends, professional worlds that overlap but don't align, and decisions that can no longer be deferred. The things that drew them to each other are the things that make this hardest.

Timeline: Mid-January (post-Globes) through April 2027.

## Chapter 1 — Sundance
*Mid-Jan – late Jan*
*POV: Dual — weighted toward her*

---

### Beat 1 — The Call
*Dual POV — mid-Jan, after Globes week*

He calls. Evening his time, afternoon hers. She hears it before he says anything — different acoustics. No echo off the high ceilings she's used to hearing in the background. A smaller room, closer walls.

You moved in.

Today. Fairfield — seven minutes from the house. She asks how the kids took it. He tells her. The twins came after school. Arrow wanted to know which room was hers. Zeppelin opened every cupboard in the kitchen and reported back that there was nothing in them. Arrow asked where her toys go. Zeppelin asked if the pool works. Both of them exploring, touching things, asking questions — and then about forty minutes in Arrow went quiet and said she wanted to go home. Said it small. Not dramatic. Just done.

He pauses. She holds the space.

JJ walked through the whole place without saying much. Hands in her pockets. Looked at the garden. Asked how long it takes to get to school from here. Asked what days they'd be there. Then sat on the couch, got on her phone, and asked for the WiFi password. When they left she just said bye. Quiet. Not cold — controlled.

She hears all of it. Doesn't fill it with reassurance. Lets him sit with what it was — his kids in a house that isn't the one he built for them, asking where they fit.

He moves on before she has to.

Then the briefing. His team sat him down about Sundance. He tells her in the dry register they use for the machinery — his PR team has given him a professional strategy document about how to behave around her ex-boyfriends.

She's quiet for a beat. Then — they gave you a document.

He says it was closer to a seminar. Two names. Rules of engagement for each. He's fairly certain there was a flow chart.

She laughs.

He gives her the RJ section first. His team flagged that RJ is at Sundance — and then the part he's actually asking about. RJ is staying at her house.

Why didn't she tell him.

She didn't know until last night. Shaun mentioned it. She honestly forgot — RJ has stayed at the house the last two years with the group. Eight bedrooms. They've done it this way because doing anything different now would be the thing that draws attention.

He's quiet. She adds — that's what his team said too, isn't it.

It is.

She tells him simply. Her and RJ were a long time ago and for her it was never serious. They made it work afterward because they have the same friends. That's it.

He takes it. Believes her. Doesn't say the thing he's carrying — how RJ looked at her at the charity event. That her read of what it was and his read of what it was may not be the same. He holds it. Not for this call.

Then Cosmo. The shift is subtle. He delivers the strategy the same way — steady, dry, relaying what his team told him. Don't avoid Cosmo. Avoidance reads as insecurity. Attend the screenings, the Q&As, the industry dinners even if Cosmo is in the room. If they cross — brief acknowledgement, professional tone, move on. If she enters a space where Cosmo is, he doesn't track her, doesn't shift, doesn't react. No comparison optics.

The words are the same professional register as the RJ section. The delivery isn't. He's funnier about RJ — the absurdity is available to him there. With Cosmo the humour thins. He's controlled where he was loose. Precise where he was dry.

She hears it. Not what he says — how the Cosmo section lands differently. RJ doesn't scare him. Something about Cosmo does.

She doesn't push. Files it.

She gives him her side. One controlled interaction with Cosmo across the whole week. Maximum. Late in her stay, not early. No second visible interaction. No repeated proximity. No overlap with Jensen in the same time window.

She doesn't tell him the other part of her briefing — that her team told her the Cosmo interaction must read externally as calm, contained, neutral. That whatever happens in that conversation, her face can't show it. That her team is managing her around the possibility that she might feel something in front of witnesses and that feeling something is the specific thing they need her not to do. She holds that. Because telling him her team thinks she might break open when she sees Cosmo is telling him something about Cosmo she's not ready to say.

The joint instruction from both teams: coexistence without comparison. They are allowed to know each other. They are not allowed to look like she's choosing between them.

He says something about how his PR team knows more about her dating history than he does. She says that's probably true for anyone who's read the Daily Mail. The register back. The ease underneath the machinery.

The call settles into the two of them — the new house, the week ahead, and the fact that Globes proved they could do this. Sundance is smaller, fewer places to hide, and two names from her past in the same ten-block radius. But they just survived the biggest week on the calendar.

### Beat 2 — The Plane
*Her POV — Thu Jan 21*

Her, Chace, Kelsey, Miles, Keleigh, Shaun, RJ on a plane to Utah. The group energy easy — luggage overhead, seats swapped, someone's already opened something they shouldn't have opened before takeoff. Third year running. Same house, same people, same week.

RJ is across the aisle. Comfortable, easy, the version of them that works — old friends who share a group and don't make it weird. She hasn't thought about him in that context in years. The three months they tried were brief enough that most people in the group have forgotten it happened. RJ hasn't forgotten. She knows that. But the dynamic is managed and has been for a long time.

Shaun is next to her at some point. Direct, the way Shaun is about everything. He's been briefed on the week — not the full PR strategy, just the shape. Jensen at a separate chalet. The playbook from Globes applied to a smaller town. Chace as the natural bridge.

Shaun asks about the house. Whether it's going to be strange — RJ in the same house, Jensen ten minutes away. She says it's fine. RJ has been at the house the last two years. Nothing is different.

Shaun looks at her. Says something about how it's different for RJ. Not accusatory — just honest. His friend. A man who was genuinely into her and got the door closed on him. Now sharing a house with her for a week while she's seeing someone else. She might be fine with it. Shaun's not sure RJ is.

She hears it. Doesn't argue. Shaun told her the same thing on a boat six months ago — that she never gave RJ a real chance. He's not re-litigating it. He's telling her to be aware of what the week looks like from RJ's side.

Then Cosmo. Shaun asks it straight — is she okay with him being there.

She says she's fine. It was five years ago.

Shaun doesn't push it the way he pushed RJ. He was there for Cosmo. He knows what it cost her. He also knows that when she says she's fine about something that isn't fine, pushing makes her close the door faster.

Chace runs through the week. Practical — who's where, what days, the venues. He bridges both sides of Sundance naturally. Same people who worked together, run into each other, say hi. Always in a group. The chalets are ten minutes apart in Deer Valley — she can walk to Jensen's. The playbook is the same as Globes. Fewer events, smaller town, fewer places to hide.

She adds the Cosmo rules from the briefing. One interaction maximum across the week. Late, not early. No overlap with Jensen in the same window. Chace and Kelsey hear it. The group is the buffer that makes all of it work.

### Beat 3 — Arrival
*Her POV — Thu Jan 21*

The chalet. Deer Valley. Eight bedrooms. Settling in — luggage, rooms claimed, the group finding the kitchen.

RJ finds his room. Easy with her the way he's been since they figured out how to be friends. Shaun watching without saying anything — he said what he said on the plane.

Jensen texts. Already there, already has events. He'll see her tonight.

She walks to his chalet after the group settles. First night. The ease from Globes week still there — the domestic rhythm they built across five nights.

## Chapter 2 — Friday
*Fri Jan 22*
*POV: Dual — weighted toward her*

### Beat 1 — The Day
*Her POV — Fri Jan 22, daytime*

Morning at the Armani-aligned suite. Brand visibility — controlled, not wandering. The group forms around midday for Main Street. Keleigh, Kelsey, her, Chace drifting in and out, RJ present as normal. Coffee, a gifting suite stop, the group photographed casually. The smallness of Park City — everyone walks past everyone.

RJ is easy in the group. The version of him she's always comfortable around. If Shaun is watching the dynamic he's not showing it today.

His day is separate. Screenings in the morning. Working lunch with his agent or producing partner. Afternoon meetings — writers, directors, the Chaos Machine development pipeline he's here for. She gets a text mid-afternoon. Something about a screening he just sat through. Their register — dry, specific, probably unflattering about something he was supposed to be impressed by. She texts back between a gifting suite and the group finding somewhere for late lunch.

The rhythm of it. Separate days, shared texts, the connection running underneath two completely different versions of Sundance.

---

### Beat 2 — The Group
*Her POV — Fri Jan 22, afternoon/early evening*

Back at the chalet. The group resetting before the evening. The noise of people getting ready in different rooms, someone's music too loud, Keleigh borrowing something.

Chace asks how the week is sitting with her so far. Not the PR version — the actual version. She says it's fine. One night down, playbook running. He says he saw Jensen briefly on Main Street between meetings. Completely separate. Nobody clocked it.

Kelsey asks about the chalet arrangement — whether it's strange, the walking back and forth. She says it's ten minutes. Kelsey says that's not what she asked.

She's honest with them. It's strange. Globes week they were in the same house. Here she's in a group chalet with eight people and he's ten minutes away and the walk between them is the part of the day she has to be most careful about. But it's working.

Someone — probably Keleigh — says something about how at some point they should be able to just go to dinner together like normal people. She says something back. Their register, the group version of it. The laughter is real. The underneath isn't funny.

### Beat 3 — Split Dinners
*Dual POV — Fri Jan 22, evening*

Evening splits. Him at an industry dinner — producers, agents, the rooms where Chaos Machine business happens. Her with the group at a social dinner. Separate worlds, running clean.

He texts her from his dinner. Something about the conversation at his table or the food or the specific energy of a room full of people trying to sell each other things. She sends something back from hers. The two of them narrating their separate evenings to each other underneath the evenings themselves.

### Beat 4 — His Chalet
*Dual POV — Fri Jan 22, late*

She walks to his chalet after the group gets back. The ten-minute walk through Deer Valley that is the most exposed part of their week and also the only part that's theirs.

The ease from Globes still there. His chalet is quiet after a day of meetings and a dinner full of people. Just them.

He tells her about his day. The screenings, who he met, what's worth pursuing. She tells him about hers — the Main Street walk, the group, the Armani suite. Two people downloading separate days to each other.

She asks about tomorrow. He runs through it — more screenings in the morning, meetings in the afternoon. Then the evening. The WME dinner — they're both on the list. First time in the same room since they got here.

He asks what she's wearing tomorrow. She asks why. He says because his PR team spent an hour telling him how to ignore her in a room of fourteen people and he needs to know how difficult she's planning to make that.

She says she hasn't decided yet. He says that's worse. She says she could wear something terrible. Make it easy for him. He says she doesn't own anything terrible. She says that's his problem, not hers.

The conversation settles. Two people at the end of a day that worked exactly as planned. Tomorrow is the first test.

### Beat 2 — Main Street
*His POV — Sat Jan 23, midday*

He exits a meeting or the IMDb studio. Main Street. She's walking with the group — Chace, Keleigh, Kelsey, Shaun, RJ. Coffee, the casual Sundance midday drift.

Brief acknowledgement. Natural, easy — of course they crossed paths, Park City is ten blocks. He keeps moving. She keeps moving. Chace bridges it without effort — a quick word to Jensen, back to the group. Exactly what the playbook looks like when it works.

But he's seen it now. RJ beside her. Comfortable, unhurried, part of the group the way someone who belongs there is part of it. Not performing. Not trying. Just there — easy in her world in a way Jensen can't be this week.

And the way RJ looks at her. The same way he looked at her at the charity event. Not obvious, not performing. But there — a man watching a woman he hasn't stopped watching. She doesn't see it. She never has.

### Beat 3 — The Chalet
*Her POV — Sat Jan 23, afternoon*

Late afternoon. Getting ready for the evening. Keleigh, Kelsey, Aoibhéann and Shaun in the main room of the chalet. Chace and Miles elsewhere.

Kelsey and Keleigh start it — opening their own press narratives first so Aoibhéann's doesn't sit alone. Kelsey gives hers: social climber, doesn't represent her Filipino roots. Keleigh gives hers: mean girl, attention seeker. Both delivered dry, both real — things actually written about them. They know the machinery because it's been pointed at them too.

Aoibhéann gives hers. The homewrecker. The gold digger who comes from more money than most of the people writing about her. The party girl. The woman with no substance whose charity raised 4.3 million dollars.

Keleigh says something about Hailey Bieber surviving way worse. Years of it. Millions of people. Still standing.

Aoibhéann says Hailey had the advantage of actually being married to the person they were attacking her over. She's getting the same treatment for a relationship nobody can even confirm exists.

Shaun says the press loves to exaggerate her life. Do they remember Julianne's birthday a couple of years ago. She had to leave early because she had a shoot the next day. Next day he sees an article and it's basically — "She arrives solo, keeps her distance all night, then makes a sudden exit after tense exchanges."

He's sitting there like — they were debating dessert. The only tension was whether they needed a second one.

She didn't even look at anyone wrong. If anything she was the nicest person there.

That's when he realised — they don't need anything to happen. They just take whatever she does and translate it into the version of her they've already decided on.

### Beat 5 — The WME Dinner
*Dual POV — Sat Jan 23, evening*

WME private dinner. Deer Valley house. Twelve to fourteen people around a table — actors, producers, directors, partners. Curated guest list. Her presence normal — WME represents people in her world. His presence expected — producer, Chaos Machine business. No coincidence needed.

They greet once. Brief, warm, professional. Same room, different clusters. She's at one end of the table. He's at the producer end — agents, a director he's been pursuing, the business conversations he's here for.

Chace moves between both sides naturally. Not babysitting. Just Chace — the man who knows everyone at the table and has a reason to talk to all of them.

The room is small enough that she can hear his laugh from across the table. He can see her without turning his head. The awareness is there — manageable but constant. Fourteen people and both of them tracking each other without looking.

No cameras inside. But arrivals are noted. Drivers confirm. The first overlap logged.

### Beat 6 — His Chalet
*Dual POV — Sat Jan 23, late*

She walks to his chalet. The ten-minute walk. Second night.

He asks how she thought it went. She says fine — they barely spoke, Chace covered the middle ground, nobody clocked anything. He says she's wrong about one thing. She asks what. He says she was managing perfectly well until he laughed at something at the other end of the table and she completely lost her train of thought mid-sentence.

She asks how he noticed that if he wasn't supposed to be watching her. He doesn't answer that.

Then he asks about RJ. Not the PR version — not the briefing, not the strategy. He saw them on Main Street today.

She says RJ was probably more into it than she was. She knows that. But it's in the past.

He says he's not sure about that.

She's quiet. He doesn't push it further. He said what he saw. She heard it. The week continues.

### Beat 5 — His Chalet
*Dual POV — Sun Jan 24, late*

She walks to his chalet. Third night. He's quieter than Friday and Saturday. She notices.

He tells her about the Garland dinner. The creative conversation — real, substantial, the kind of meeting that justifies the whole week. She asks about Garland's work, what Jensen's thinking for Chaos Machine. The professional conversation is genuine.

Then he tells her Garland mentioned her. The Devs premiere. The criticism she made about the visual approach. Garland remembered it years later.

She's surprised. She remembers the conversation but not as anything significant. She pushed back on something she thought was wrong. Garland pushed back harder. She held her ground. That was it.

He asks about the premiere. She tells him — London, 2020. She went with Cosmo. It was his world, his people. She was the girlfriend at the screening.

She says it simply. No weight on it. But Jensen now has the image — her in a room with Cosmo, in Cosmo's world, holding her own against a director and being remembered for it. Before Jensen knew she existed.

He doesn't tell her about seeing Cosmo in the lobby. The honesty commitment sitting alongside the thing he's not saying.

Not on his schedule. Megan doesn't know. His decision.

He goes deliberately. Cosmo doing a Q&A — his film, his process, his element. Jensen sits toward the back.

Cosmo on stage is everything the profile described. Intense, articulate, private but present when the work requires it. Talks about craft in a way that's specific and unperformative. The indie film world is his home.

Jensen walks out knowing what she responded to. Not just the look — the mind. The depth. The complete disinterest in visibility or performance. And the doubt: am I anything like what she actually wants.

### Beat 4 — The Honesty Card
*Dual POV — Mon Jan 25, midday*

She finds out before he can tell her. Someone mentioned it — a photo from the Q&A with Cosmo's name visible and Jensen recognisable in the audience, or Chace heard from someone, or Kelsey mentions it.

She calls him. Pulls the honesty commitment. Direct. Why did you go to that particular Q&A. Why didn't you mention it.

He's honest. Mostly. Tells her about seeing Cosmo in the lobby yesterday — the physical reality of him. Tells her about Garland and the Devs anecdote — that someone he respects told him a story about his girlfriend at an event with another man, and she was brilliant in it. That he wanted to know more about the man she was with for over two years.
