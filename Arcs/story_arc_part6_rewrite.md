**11A (Sat Dec 18 evening)**

East Norwalk rental. Evening.

Aoibhéann sits at the dining table, the entire surface taken over as her Kavanagh “Dark Mode” workstation. She reviews high-resolution retouching selects from the Dec 10–12 brand shoot.

Jensen walks in. He kicks his boots off into the centre of the narrow hallway, cluttering the path. He heads straight to the fridge, opens it, stares inside.

“Did you not have a chance to restock before we got back?”

She keeps her eyes on the screen. “No. I’ll go shopping tomorrow. Cassidy only got essentials for the morning.”

He closes the fridge. She stays at the table.

Jensen says, “Danneel saw the coffee shop photo. She sent the forum link to me while I was in the car from LGA.”

Aoibhéann slowly closes her laptop lid until it clicks. She places her palms flat on the top casing and stares at the empty space where her work was.

Jensen looks at the boots still in the middle of the hallway. “I should probably burn those boots. They’re more famous than I am at this point.”

She says, “Don’t. The fans would just track the smoke.”

She gets up, picks up his boots from the centre of the hallway and moves them to the side. Jensen goes out to the car and brings in his bag.

Aoibhéann moves to the kitchen island. She pulls up a delivery app on her phone and chooses something light to fit his filming diet. Jensen starts unpacking essentials from his bag — iPad for scripts, toiletries.

Jensen says, “I told Danneel I’d be over at six-thirty for the breakfast run. I’ll set the alarm for six.”

She keeps scrolling the app. “Six is fine. I have loads to do before the call with the Content team at 10.”

The takeout arrives. They eat at the kitchen island. Conversation stays on immediate logistics of the next 48 hours.

Jensen turns off the downstairs lights and the porch light overlooking the harbour. They move upstairs together.

In bed she says, “I did remember to stock up on condoms while I was at CVS this morning. So I remembered the important things.”

He laughs. “You told me off when I got some from a shop in Manhattan instead of ordering online.”

They make use of a condom.

Afterwards she says, “I’m getting used to having you every night.”

He replies, “Yes, but every time after a long stretch it gets harder to fall asleep without you.”

They are together at the rental for the night.
