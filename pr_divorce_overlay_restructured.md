## [TENSION]: The Fundamental Problem

PR requires patience. Gradual normalisation 12–18 months. No spikes.
Relationship requires presence. Opposite coasts. Schedules overlap days not weeks. Every month in Phase 1–2 is another month of phone calls and borrowed windows.
Divorce timeline controls everything. If finalises June, strategy works. If delays — every phase shifts, pressure increases.

They can survive the public side. What they can't survive indefinitely is a life built in the gaps, gated by a legal process they don't fully control, waiting for permission to exist.

Part Four engine: not the hiding. The waiting.
