## [WOUND]: The Cosmo Parallel — Part Four

### What the Cosmo Conversation Gave Her

### Cosmo vs Jensen — The Inversion
With Cosmo: I'm invisible in his real life. He doesn't show up to my world.
With Jensen: I can't access his real life. He shows up to mine but I can't show up to his.

### Why Jensen Is Worse Than Cosmo
Cosmo's real failure wasn't shutting her out — she misread what he was offering. He gave her the private life (mum's 60th, best friend's wedding). Didn't give professional visibility.

Jensen can't give her either. No family dinners. No birthdays. No meeting parents. No house when kids are there. Professional visibility exists (everyone knows) but she can't be present for his actual professional life.

She has the professional association Cosmo never gave. She doesn't have the private access Cosmo actually gave.

### The Imbalance
She is letting him in. Met friends at Sundance. Declan. Cameron and Adam. Family at Easter. Every door in her life open. His doors closed — logistics, kids, geography, Danneel entanglement with every friendship.

### Self-Knowledge Makes It Harder

## [ATTACHMENT]: How They're Wired

### Aoibhéann — Fearful-Avoidant
Wants closeness, expects loss. Moves toward connection with real courage (kissed him first, engineered Toronto, said "I love you" unplanned at the treehouse, opened every door — friends, family, Easter, Brigid reveal). But when connection threatens to repeat a wound, she switches off, leaves, or manages. She doesn't shrink — the one exception is his children, where the Stephanie wound locks her.

Origins: Brigid's death at 13 (unconditional parent gone). Jack present but disappointing. Stephanie managing not loving. Connollys requiring her to earn belonging she had by birth. Love learned as conditional and temporary.

The cycle in her relationships: Alex (loved him, he cheated — left her with the evening-mapping system she still carries). Cosmo (she shut him out as much as he failed to engage — learned Connolly behaviour). Nicholas (overcorrection to safe and stable, bored her). Chaotic relationships then overcorrections to boring ones. Canyon house bought after Cosmo specifically to stop the pattern of moving.

With Jensen: proposes structured honesty calls because she recognises they defer hard conversations then choose each other over talking (naming her own avoidance). Held back during fashion month (he felt her pulling away). Let "bad person" gaps go in February because pushing felt like being Stephanie. Maps his evenings by when the phone rings (Alex system applied to Jensen).

### Jensen — Secure Baseline with Anxious Activation
Stable foundation: Texas family, grounded values, consistent across thirty years, twenty years with Danneel. Loyalty central. Shows up.

But this situation activates insecurity he has no precedent for. He hasn't navigated early attraction in twenty years. Doesn't trust his own reading of her feelings — sees something, questions whether it's reality or projection.

The insecurity is real and specific:
- Cosmo scares him. He has no framework for what she responded to in Cosmo and the evidence suggests it might be something he isn't.
- Her world of close male friendships (Shaun, Miles, Chace, RJ) — younger, more culturally aligned with her, from her world. Produces "the specific discomfort of not knowing."
- Her social world is entirely different from his. Chace is the sharpest evidence: worked with Jensen for years, like each other enough, never became friends because they're too different. Chace and Aoibhéann have been close friends for years. This repeats across her whole circle — he's crossed paths with many of these people through the industry and never connected with any of them. They're not his people.
- Easter: not legible in her family's room. TV, Texas Tech didn't finish. Ashley legible. He isn't.
- Danneel's question (Ch9 B1): did she influence his decision to leave. He hasn't told Aoibhéann. That question touches whether the foundation is real or whether he was running toward her.

Information management is his anxious-protective pattern: held "bad person" for two months, parcelled financials, didn't tell her about Danneel's question. Withholds not because he fears closeness but because he fears the information will drive her away. The Part Two pattern: "Information arrives late, incomplete, or from someone other than him."
