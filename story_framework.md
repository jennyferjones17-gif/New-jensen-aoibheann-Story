## PROGRESSION MODEL

### Core Story Progression (Parts 1 → 6)

- **Part 1:** Attraction builds through the charity work. Neither acts. Ends with the first hook-up after filming wraps.
- **Part 2:** Event execution. Physical acted on, emotional weight compounds. Public breaks via festival photos. Filing, date fight, move-out.
- **Part 3:** Reconnection after break. Testing whether this works.
- **Part 4:** Introduction of Aoibhéann to Jensen's inner world (friends, family, kids on the horizon). Fashion month Milan shows the pattern that will repeat.
- **Part 5:** Trial — proximity, counsellor work, first kid meetings, Shaun threshold, Path B dinner, East Norwalk rental.
- **Part 6:** Distance. Does the relationship exist when proximity doesn't?
- **Part 7:** Post-finalisation, post-launch. Still held for planning.

### The Attraction-Connection Engine (how it keeps firing)

- **Stage:** External world makes the case their worlds are too different.
- **Response:** Something internal arrives that quietly makes the counter-argument.
- **Pattern:** Neither engineers it. Accumulation is the engine.
- **What changes over parts:** the mechanism holds but the stakes of each counter-argument rise. Part 1 stakes: should I even acknowledge this? Part 6 stakes: can this survive distance?

### PR Trajectory (Parts 2 → 6)

- **Part 2 festival photos:** First public exposure. Rumours, not confirmation.
- **Sundance January 2027:** Mainstream pickup. Timeline debates. First major spike.

- **Part 6 January finalisation:** Joint statement drops. Accumulation meets catalyst → the preload pays off.
- **Constant:** the strategy of distance and Path B is working until it suddenly isn't. False confidence builds into the exposure.

## The January/February Dynamic — The Background Threat

Something happens in late January NYC before the separation is public. A kiss or an almost moment. Neither addresses it.

The February Toronto meeting — professional reason, legitimate purpose, dinner that runs too long in a city that is neither of theirs. They pull back again.

These two moments exist as private history before the March announcement. When the announcement goes out it recontextualises January and February for both of them without either of them saying so out loud.

If the timeline ever becomes public — separation announced first week of March, something happened in January — the gap is not enormous. That is the specific background threat that runs underneath everything from January through the event and beyond. Not that they caused the separation. But that the timing is not entirely clean and both of them know it.

## The Charity

A major fundraising event focused on mental health — specifically access, visibility, and acceptance.

She became involved with the existing organisation a couple of years ago — it was established but losing wider appeal. She stepped in as a more active member, widened the donor base through her own connections and selectively through her father's, and brought the entertainment world in through her own relationships. She did not found it but she is making it hers through results. The festival is her biggest project to date.

He is the headline ambassador. The charity is her world, her authority, her terms. He fits into her schedule, not the other way around.

Both arrived at this cause through the same experience from different directions — watching someone they cared about struggle with their mental health while feeling completely helpless. Neither could fix it. Neither has fully resolved that helplessness.

That shared experience is the first real point of connection between them. It arrives before either of them is ready for it.

For him, his involvement is rooted in loyalty and proximity — specifically Jared Padalecki's openness about his struggles with depression. He shows up because it matters to someone he loves and because he has watched firsthand what silence costs people.

For her, the cause comes from accumulated witness across three worlds — the old money world where difficulty is managed privately and never named, the Irish world where mental health carries a specific cultural shame, and the fashion and modelling industry where the relationship with mental health is devastating and largely unspoken. None of these are her own story. All of them are real and specific and something she can speak to with genuine authority.

**Press and competition cycle:**

Every competition announcement, winner reveal, and behind the scenes preview is a press moment. The public watches them associated with each other for months before the event through this cycle.

## The Contact Structure

**Early months — through teams:**
Initial contact is managed through representatives. Professional, formal, logistics focused.

**Mid period — co-recruitment:**
Both are actively recruiting other participants — celebrity players for the baseball game, Legends, panel contributors, artists for the concert. He has relationships she needs. She has relationships he doesn't. This creates legitimate direct contact well before the intensive phase. Messages and calls that start about securing a specific person and occasionally run longer than either of them planned.

**By the intensive phase:**
A communication rhythm already exists. They know each other's working style. A familiarity has arrived quietly through months of practical collaboration. Neither engineered it. The charity built it for them.

## The Charity — Her Role

She is the vision, the relationships, the fundraising engine, the public face. She has a professional executive director who manages operations. The creative direction, the donor relationships, the talent relationships — those are hers.

Using her father's connections selectively for the donor world is the one place the thing she built isn't entirely independent. She has feelings about that she hasn't fully resolved.

## The Attraction and Connection

**How it starts:**

He is drawn to her almost immediately. Doesn't fully understand why. She is not performing for him — she is focused on the work and he fits around her, which is unusual enough to get his attention in a way that goes beyond the obvious.

She notices him physically after a short while and is mildly surprised by it. Files it immediately under irrelevant. She is focused on the charity and on sorting her life out. She has dated too many people who were charming, good looking, and red flags simultaneously. He is attractive, she understands why he has the following he does, and she moves on.

Both are subconsciously drawn to the other without fully acknowledging it.

**How it builds:**

Moments of genuine connection accumulate gradually across the timeline. Neither engineers them. They arrive uninvited — through the cause itself, through what surfaces about why each of them is really there, through values recognised, losses recognised, the specific helplessness of watching someone struggle and not knowing what to do.

Every time the external world makes the case that their worlds are too different, something internal quietly makes the counter argument. Each time. Without fail. The accumulation is the engine.

**How it moves:**

They act on the physical thinking it will release the tension and simplify things. It doesn't. The emotional weight becomes undeniable after. What they told themselves this was stops being a story either of them can maintain.

The slow burn is not will they or won't they. It is the growing impossibility of pretending they already know what this is.

## The Connection Engine — Recurring Beats

The specific things that keep pulling them back toward each other despite everything external working against it:

- The value of uncomplicated family — people connected simply because they are family, with no agenda attached
- The helplessness of watching someone struggle with mental health and not knowing what to do
- More to be identified as the story develops

Each time the distance between their worlds feels insurmountable, one of these moments arrives and quietly dismantles that case. Neither of them asks for it. Neither of them can fully defend against it.

### The Stephanie Fear — Corrected Understanding

**What it is NOT:**
- NOT about meeting Stephanie (she was 3 when Stephanie married Jack — she can't remember).
- NOT about age 13 or the arrival specifically.

**What it IS:**
- Stephanie simply EXISTED in the space where Aoibhéann's family should have been.
- She didn't hate Stephanie for being cruel. She hated her for being there.
- Jack remarried within two years of divorcing Brigid. To a child that reads as replacement.
- What Aoibhéann felt was grief dressed as hatred.
- At 35 the hatred has burned out into indifference with an old scar underneath.

**The Parallel to Jensen's Children:**
- Jensen's separation was eighteen months ago. Her father remarried within two years. The timing is the same. Closer, even.
- She can do everything right — be careful, quiet, not try too hard — and Arrow might still look at her the way she looked at Stephanie. Not for doing something wrong. For being there.

**Cameron's Deeper Fear:**
- Aoibhéann will be so determined not to replicate Stephanie's role that she will consistently put herself last. Will deny her own needs, timeline, wants, to make sure Jensen's children never feel what she felt.
- She will be the anti-Stephanie so completely that she disappears in the process.
- And she isn't prepared for the possibility that his children might resent her anyway — regardless of how carefully she handles it.
