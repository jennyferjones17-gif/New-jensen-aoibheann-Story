## INTERACTION LOGIC

How the family operates as a system.

### Public vs Private Operating Modes
- **Public:** Ambient, managed, charity-performative. Curated to serve the family image. The privacy itself is strategic.
- **Private:** Direct, competitive, confrontational. Modelled on Kennedy family dynamics. Commentary happens in real time. Opinions are not softened.
- **Consequence:** what Aoibhéann can't say in public rooms gets said bluntly in private ones — and she hears all of it.

### Power Flow
- **Joe Sr → patriarch generation.** Founding authority.
- **Jack → US patriarch now.** Charm + threat activation mechanism. Becomes active threat when family closes ranks.
- **Rose / Daniel Kennedy → establishment senior branch.** Most establishment within the family.
- **Caroline Prescott → cousin-generation tone-setter.** Cold correctness. Sets the treatment register other cousins follow.
- **Stephanie → social manager.** Speaks as family insider who owns and manages the space. NOT outsider.
- **Joe III → first to assess Jensen** when he enters the family world.

### Stephanie's Specific Position
- Second wife. Socially impeccable. Surface warm. Focused entirely on perception.
- Manages the house, the staff, the social schedule — the Connolly-world version of traditional woman (managerial, not hands-on).
- Did not hate Aoibhéann as a child — simply existed where Aoibhéann's family should have been.
- Privately sees the Jensen dynamic clearly ("you wouldn't need it if it weren't for Jensen" at Thanksgiving Greenwich).

### Connolly Money Mechanism
- Every Connolly marriage has money underneath the structure.
- The person who pays, the person who receives, the debt never named but always counted.
- Jack's world runs on it. Aoibhéann swore she'd never be in that position.
- This is why she refuses Jack's money and Jensen's money in the same week. Not because Jensen is doing what Connollys do — because the effect is the same even when intent differs.

### The Jensen Parallel (nobody says out loud)
- Connolly family eventually pushed Brigid out for not fitting.
- Family now mobilising against Jensen for the same instinct — wrong fit, not appropriate, not what they would choose.
- Aoibhéann is in Brigid's position. Jensen is in hers. Jack is watching it happen and doing what his family did to him.
- He hasn't let himself see that clearly. Too expensive to look at directly.

### Critical Continuity Rule

- **Never frame her entry into the family as beginning at 13.**

## The Greenwich Estate — The East Coast Family Seat

### How Old East Coast Families Work

The estate belongs to the family. It is lived in by one branch.

One branch becomes the "seat holder" — the primary resident couple who act as hosts, custodians, and the default centre of the family. In old East Coast Irish Catholic families this is typically the eldest son, especially if he is in the core business and stayed on the East Coast. It is not a rule, but it is the most common pattern — the most stable sibling, the one in the core business, the one who stayed.

The estate is not shared living. Other siblings do not live there full-time and do not treat it as a second home casually. They visit. They stay in assigned rooms. They return to their own lives.

Permanent residence is responsibility as much as privilege. The resident branch manages staff, hosts holidays, maintains the property, and represents the family socially. It is not just privilege — it is duty.

### Who Lives There

**Joe Connolly is the seat holder.** Eldest son. Banking side (core business). East Coast-based. Stable. He and Margaret live there permanently. Their children — Joe III, Grace, Ronan — grew up there. They have their own childhood bedrooms in the main house. This is their home.

**Jack does NOT live there.** He built his own branch — LA, entertainment. He has a wing in the main house (a permanent family allocation for the second son), but he is not the resident custodian. His children visit. They do not live there.

**Other siblings — all East Coast, none resident:**
- Kathleen — Connecticut, nearby.
- Thomas — Upper East Side, New York.
- Rose — Upper East Side, New York.

All close. None resident. They come for events and holidays. They go home.

Private waterfront compound, approximately 15 acres, Greenwich, Connecticut. Cobb Island / Belle Haven tier. Direct Long Island Sound frontage. Large continuous lawn to water. Fully concealed from road. Multiple structures.

**How it feels:** Not flashy, not newly built, not showy. Quiet, established, inherited. "This house has always been here." Seen in finance families and legacy East Coast families.
