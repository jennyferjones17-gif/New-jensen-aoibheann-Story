## Chapter 1 — Departure + Separate Orbits
*Window: Aug 17 – early September 2027*

**Operating principle:** Part Five tested the relationship on proximity. Part Six tests whether it exists when proximity is gone. Nothing pauses — the divorce progresses, the kids continue processing, her career expands, his filming consumes him.

A coffee place in Malibu. She's early. He arrives ten minutes after. She tells him she'll probably get a call from her PR team about this — her and a male friend in public, alone. Tells him that is, incidentally, the whole fucking problem. He half-laughs. He doesn't fully.

They talk for a while after. Work — his season, her beauty line in shape. He asks about Cameron's wedding. She tells him. They don't circle back to what's between them.

Evening. Jensen calls. First full day of filming — Block 1 started yesterday. He tells her how the day went, what worked, what didn't. She tells him about Shaun — the whole thing, start to finish. He listens. Tells her he thinks it went as well as it was going to. She tells him she knows.

She hopes it works out. Means it. But she can't be part of it.
